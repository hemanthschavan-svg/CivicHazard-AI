How CivicHazard AI Works

A transparent process from reporting to resolution.

STEP 01

Report the Issue

Upload a photograph, describe the problem and share its location.

STEP 02

AI Image Analysis

AI examines the photograph to identify supported civic problems.

STEP 03

Department Action

The complaint is routed to the responsible department for action.

STEP 04

Resolution Verification

Completion evidence is checked before the complaint is marked resolved.
